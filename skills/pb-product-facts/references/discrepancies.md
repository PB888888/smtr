# Discrepancy register

Where two Paddock Blade sources disagree, the conflict is recorded here rather than
silently resolved. The instruction is *"if two sources conflict, do not guess —
prefer the most current country-specific source and flag the discrepancy"*, and a
register is how that survives past the moment it was noticed.

Each entry states what conflicts, which source wins, and what to actually say.

---

## 1. Original Paddock Blade colour pricing (UK)

| Source | Says |
| --- | --- |
| FAQ document, "Why is the red one cheaper?" | Standard Red is the popular option; all other colours are made to order and cost more to produce. |
| **UK Shopify store, live 2026-08-17** | Cherry Red **£479** and Stealth Black **£479** — identical. |

**Resolution:** Shopify wins — it is the current, country-specific source. In the
UK both colours are the same price.

**What to say:** if asked, both UK colours are £479. Do not reach for the FAQ's
made-to-order explanation, because a customer who then sees identical prices will
reasonably wonder what else is wrong.

**Note:** the FAQ answer may still hold in other markets, where made-to-order
colours could carry a premium. It cannot be checked from here. Do not apply the UK
finding to another market either — that is the same error in reverse.

## 2. Round pens in the UK

| Source | Says |
| --- | --- |
| Business Bible §11.1, UK product categories | Round Pens listed among known UK categories |
| **UK Shopify store, live 2026-08-17** | No active round pen product among the 20 active items |

**Resolution:** Shopify wins on availability. The Business Bible is a strategy
document describing categories and intent, not a live SKU list — it says so itself,
warning that availability "must always be checked against the current regional SKU
matrix".

**What to say:** do not offer round pens as available in the UK. If a customer asks
specifically, escalate rather than saying they do not exist — they may be
unpublished, out of stock, or sold by enquiry.

## 3. "Lifetime warranty" versus 10 years

| Source | Says |
| --- | --- |
| FAQ, internal column | "Standard lifetime warranty — 10 Years" |
| FAQ, public columns | "The lifetime warranty is the lifetime of the paddock blade product which we deem to be 10 years from the date of purchase" |
| Marketing copy across sources | "lifetime warranty", frequently without the qualifier |

**Resolution:** not a true conflict — the company defines "lifetime" as 10 years.
But the *unqualified* marketing use is a live risk, because a customer who bought on
"lifetime" and is refused in year 11 has a reasonable grievance.

**What to say:** when the warranty is the subject of the question, give the
qualifier: 10 years from the date of purchase. Never repeat "lifetime" bare in an
answer about warranty length. This is not undermining the marketing — it is
preventing a complaint eleven years out.

## 4. Product naming — "Aurora" solariums

| Source | Says |
| --- | --- |
| Business Bible §11.2 and §11.3, and social copy | "Aurora solariums" / "Aurora Solarium" |
| **UK Shopify store** | "Paddock Blade Deluxe Aluminium Horse Solarium" — no "Aurora" |

**Resolution:** unresolved. It appears the Aurora name is used in the US and
Canadian markets, and possibly in marketing globally, while the UK store uses the
Deluxe Aluminium name. Both may be the same product under different names, or they
may be different products.

**What to say:** use the name from the customer's own store. Do **not** tell a UK
customer about an "Aurora" solarium, and do not assume a US customer's Aurora is
the UK Deluxe.

## 5. Payload capacity — units

| Source | Says |
| --- | --- |
| FAQ | "up to 500kgs / half a tonne" |
| Social copy | "1100 lb tow capacity" / "can carry up to 1100lbs" |

**Resolution:** consistent. 1,100 lb ≈ 499 kg. No action needed.

Recorded because it *looks* like a conflict at a glance, and someone will otherwise
re-investigate it.

## 6. Years in business and years tested

| Source | Says |
| --- | --- |
| FAQ, older row | "tried and tested for 7+ years across UK, Australia and New Zealand" |
| FAQ, another row | "vigorously tested for over 8 years" |
| FAQ, newer row | "tried and tested for 10 years across UK, Australia, New Zealand, America, Canada and Europe" |
| FAQ, brand questions | In business "10+ years" |

**Resolution:** the document has been updated over time and older figures were left
in place. Use **10+ years in business** and **tested for 10 years** across the six
markets — the most recent and most inclusive statement.

**What to say:** "over 10 years". Avoid the specific smaller numbers; they read as
less impressive and are simply out of date.

---

## How to add to this register

When a conflict is found, record: the sources, what each says, which wins under the
source-priority order, and the practical instruction. The last part is the one that
matters — a register that documents a conflict without saying what to tell the
customer has only done half the job.
