## Payment

All major credit and debit cards, bank transfer, PayPal, and **Klarna**.

Klarna specifics, since customers ask: pay in 3 interest-free instalments. The
first is collected when the order is confirmed; the second and third at 30 and 60
days. There is no delay to delivery. Customers can track the schedule in the Klarna
app.

## Discrepancies affecting this market

**"Why is the red one cheaper?"** The FAQ answers this — standard red is the
popular option, other colours are made to order and cost more. **That is no longer
true in the UK.** Cherry Red and Stealth Black are both £479 live. Do not use the
FAQ's answer here; if a customer asks, both colours are the same price.

**Round pens** appear in the Business Bible's UK category list but there is **no
active round pen product** in the UK store. Do not offer them as available.

Both are logged in `discrepancies.md`.
